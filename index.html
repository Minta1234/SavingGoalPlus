📊

260° Dynamic Arc Progress Gauge

Unlike boring linear progress bars, SavingGoalPlus features an animated 260-degree vector arc gauge. It visually shifts through 3 intelligent color tiers: Amber (≤30%) for warmup, Cyan (31–60%) for steady momentum, and Emerald Green (>60%) for victory.

🧮

Mathematical Rate Projection Engine

Know exactly how much you need to set aside. Our formula automatically projects Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, and Monthly required savings rates based on your custom target date and remaining balance.

🎉

Interactive Confetti Particle Physics

Financial milestones deserve celebration! Hit 100% on any goal to trigger a full-screen 2D particle physics explosion with gravity, angular momentum, and celebratory chimes.

⏰

Disciplined Daily Reminder Alarms

Never lose track of your financial habits. Configure custom scheduled notifications via Android AlarmManager (with device reboot persistence) and Apple UNUserNotificationCenter.

🔒

100% Offline, Zero Ads, Zero Subscriptions

Your financial data never touches an external server. Everything lives in local SQLite/JSON storage with instant one-click JSON backup export/import and CSV spreadsheet extraction.
